ABOUT THIS ATLAS

Read the data in context.

Exploratory geochemistry from the thesis project and associated regional collections.

01 / MEASUREMENTS

Reported concentrations

Displayed values must be traceable to the final sample-level table used for the thesis figures. One element will appear once in the selector. The isotope, gas mode, selected dilution and source record belong in measurement details. No raw laboratory concentration is published here.

02 / QUALITY

Thesis reporting decisions

The thesis describes blank corrections when blanks constitute 5–20% of a cation concentration and exclusion above 20%, with median blanks used where field blanks were missing. It also describes choosing reanalysis dilutions separately for affected ions. These decisions must be reflected in the final reporting table; automated flags from a separate working database are not treated as thesis approval.

03 / STATISTICS

Filters define the summary

Averages and medians use finite reported values after filters. Qualified nonnumeric results remain in the tables and downloads, and are excluded from numerical summaries. Standard deviation uses n − 1 and is unavailable for one result. Latest uses documented collection dates and averages same-day results. CV is standard deviation / |mean| × 100. Unknown collection dates are never replaced with analysis dates.

04 / GEOGRAPHY

Locations and interpretation

Site locations and the groundwater-divide interpretation are documented with their sources. Approximate locations are identified in site details. A groundwater divide is an interpretation that may vary with hydraulic conditions.
